About PodcastAI

Transform any document into a natural, engaging two-host podcast conversation — ready to listen or share in minutes.

What is PodcastAI?

PodcastAI takes your documents — PDFs, reports, essays, presentations, or plain text — and turns them into a fully voiced podcast. Two AI hosts discuss the content naturally, covering the key ideas without you having to read a single word.

It's perfect for catching up on research papers, sharing knowledge with your team, or making dense content accessible and enjoyable.

What can it handle?

You can upload PDF files, Word documents, plain text files, and PowerPoint presentations. Files up to 10 MB are supported. The system is built to handle long, dense documents and automatically filters out irrelevant content like headers, page numbers, and boilerplate text.

Voices and languages

You can choose from over 20 different voices, previewing each one before generating. Podcasts can be generated in 15 languages including English, Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and more. Speaker names appear in the script so you always know who is talking.

Your podcasts, your data

Every podcast you generate is saved to your account and accessible from the history sidebar. You can rename, revisit, or delete any podcast at any time. You can also generate a shareable link — anyone with the link can listen, no account required.

Help & FAQ

Everything you need to get started and make the most of PodcastAI.

How to use PodcastAI

1

Create an account

Sign up with your name, email, and a password. Your podcast history is saved to your account so you can revisit or share anything you've made.

2

Go to the Dashboard

Once signed in, head to the Dashboard. A status indicator at the top will confirm the service is ready. If it shows "Server not connected", please wait a moment and click Retry.

3

Pick your voices and language

Choose a voice for each of the two podcast hosts. Click the Preview button to hear a short sample before deciding. Then select your language — English is selected by default.

4

Upload your document

Drag and drop a PDF, Word document, plain text file, or PowerPoint presentation onto the upload area. Files up to 10 MB are supported.

5

Generate and listen

Click Generate podcast. The progress bar will track the stages. Depending on the length of your document, this may take a few minutes. When done, you can read the script, play the audio, and download or share both.

Frequently asked questions

What file types can I upload?▾
You can upload PDF (.pdf), Word (.docx), plain text (.txt), and PowerPoint (.pptx) files. The maximum file size is 10 MB. Scanned image-only PDFs may not extract well unless the service has OCR enabled.
How long does generation take?▾
Short documents (1–5 pages) typically take 1–3 minutes. Longer or denser documents can take 5–10 minutes. You will see a progress bar while the podcast is being created. Please keep the tab open during generation.
Can I choose different voices for each speaker?▾
Yes. You can select any voice for Speaker 1 and a different one for Speaker 2. Use the Preview buttons to hear a sample. If you prefer, tick "Use same voice for both speakers" to keep it consistent.
Which languages are supported?▾
PodcastAI supports 15 languages: English, Hindi, Telugu, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Arabic, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Italian, Russian, Bengali, and Tamil. The podcast script is automatically translated into your chosen language before audio is generated.
Can I share my podcast with someone?▾
Yes. After generation, click the Share button to get a public link. Anyone with the link can listen to the podcast and read the script — they do not need an account.
Where are my podcasts saved?▾
All podcasts are saved to your account and listed in the sidebar on the Dashboard. You can click any item to reload it, rename it, or delete it. History is tied to your account, so it will be there next time you sign in.
Can I download the audio?▾
Yes. On the Audio tab of any generated podcast, click the Download button to save the audio as a WAV file. You can also download the script as a text file from the Script tab.
The service shows "Server not connected" — what do I do?▾
This means the backend service is not reachable. If you are running PodcastAI yourself, make sure the server is started. Then click the Retry connection button on the Dashboard. If you are using a hosted version and the problem persists, please contact us.
Voices didn't load — what should I do?▾
Voices load automatically when the service connects. If the voice dropdowns are empty, try clicking Retry connection on the Dashboard. If the problem persists, the voice service may be temporarily unavailable — try again in a few minutes.
The audio generated but it is silent — why?▾
This is rare but can happen if the voice service had a temporary issue during generation. Try generating again. If it keeps happening, try switching to a different voice from the dropdown and generating again.
The translation sounds unnatural — can I fix it?▾
Translation quality depends on the language and the complexity of the document. For best results with non-English languages, keep your source document in simple, clear language. Some technical terms may not translate perfectly, but conversational content usually sounds natural.
Is there a limit on how many podcasts I can make?▾
There is no hard limit on the number of podcasts you can generate. However, very long or complex documents take more time and resources, so please allow generation to fully complete before starting another.
